## A · Voiceover explainer, every line visualised (the 40k-like one)

- **Format.** Phone handheld at a monitor in a dark room. Static title "CLAUDE JUST KILLED IT" plus crossed-out After Effects logo vs Claude logo pinned above the screen for all 98s. The "real screen in a real room" look makes it read as proof, not an ad.
- **One beat per sentence.** Every voiceover line gets its own visual, timed to the words: "you can create MOTION GRAPHICS" (huge type cropped by the frame), "And no —" (a node diagram CLAUDE → MCP SERVER → AFTER EFFECTS with a dot travelling along it), "This is CLAUDE OPUS 5.5" (orange glowing type), "Instead of controlling After Effects" (an easing curve in a graph-editor panel).
- **Accent-word typography.** Sentence in white, one key word in orange: "After **Effects**", "Claude doesn't actually **generate** an **MP4** directly". The accent word types or colours in as it's spoken.
- **Hard colour slams** for punctuation: "AND" / "WHERE" / "CRAZY." each fill the screen in black on orange, one word per beat, the only hard cuts in the piece.
- **Diagrams that build.** Boxes, dashed connectors, an ✕ on the "no direct path" line, a file icon for output.mp4. Pipeline shown as a film strip of frames scrolling ("frame by frame").
- **Mode switch for contrast.** At 0:54 the dark grid world flips to a white paper "FORM 2-B · PRODUCTION PIPELINE" document that gets filled in and struck through in red. A dictionary entry "pro·ce·dur·al (adj.)" in serif. At 1:22 a "FORM 9" multiple-choice: "Does this replace After Effects? ☐ yes ☐ no ☒ not really".
- **Live-edit UI.** A preview panel with a shape morphing (circle → square) next to a prompt list ("Make this transition smoother." → `transition: cubic-bezier...`), showing cause and effect.
- **Ending.** An arc from "idea" to "a working animation", then "PRETTY INSANE." in glowing orange, then fade to a single dot.
- **System.** Dark navy + fine grid + orange accent + white. Geometric sans (bold) for headlines, mono for labels, a serif for the dictionary moment.

## B · "Claude motion reel" showreel

- **HUD.** "CLAUDE · MOTION REEL – 2026" top-left, chapter top-right ("02 – EASING"), timecode + "60 FPS" bottom-left, "128 BPM ■□□□ BAR 3/8" bottom-right with a square stepping on every beat. Progress line along the bottom.
- **Chapters.** 01 Identity: a star burst grows out of a dot inside a thin circle, then a hard cut to "CLAUDE / *motion designer*" on orange. 02 Easing: "*Six ways to get from A to B.*" with six balls racing on rails (linear, ease-in-out, expo-out, back-out, elastic, bounce), each with its curve thumbnail. 03 Morphing: a cream shape morphs circle → triangle → star → blob → square on electric blue, ringed by satellites that morph with it, plus readouts ("MORPH CIRCLE → TRIANGLE · SPRING k=2.6"). 04 Systems: a Truchet tile pattern that ripples colour. 05 Depth: a 3D wireframe dot-sphere. 06 Kinetic type: EASE / IN. / EASE / OUT. / NEVER / LINEAR. one word per beat, each on a new colour (orange, black, cream, blue, lime). 07 Fin: the star returns as the logo mark next to CLAUDE.
- **Motion blur** on every fast move: words smear horizontally on entry ("NEVER"), letters blur as they snap in.
- **Repeat-word echo.** NEVER duplicates into stacked outlined rows behind itself.

- **Story, not features.** VO: "It starts with something you made. Tap once, you're open. T-shirts, shoes, jackets. Somewhere, someone found you. Sell what you make. And hear your first sale. Shopify." Each line maps to one product state.

## Shared language across all four

1. **Continuity over cuts.** A dot becomes a ring, a ring becomes a wipe, a pill becomes a notification, a letter becomes a circle. Hard cuts only for one-word punctuation beats.
2. **A HUD frame** (corner brackets, mono labels, chapter "NN / NN", timecode, beat counter, progress line) makes it look engineered on purpose.
3. **Type pairing.** Ultra-heavy grotesque in caps + serif italic for one soft word + mono for labels. Accent colour on one word per line, sometimes with an underline sweep.
4. **Colour fields** that change every beat (B, D), or one brand glow on near-black (A, C).
5. **Real motion blur** on fast moves, springs with small overshoot, nothing floaty.
6. **Product UI as the hero** (C, D): believable cards, live counters, a cursor that clicks, a notification as the payoff.
7. **Every visual lands on a word or a beat.**

## What RezFlo takes from this

- C's structure is the template for a RezFlo product spot: "It starts with a phone ringing at 7:42 PM… RezFlo picks up… table for 4 at 8… and hear your first booking." The payoff is a notification: "New booking · Table for 4 · 8:00 PM".
- Glow in brand purple on near-black (A, C) for standalone ads; colour-field cuts (B, D) for punctuation.
- A/C-style accent-word captions for talking-head ads: sentence in white, the money word in purple with an underline sweep.
- D's render-queue card becomes a RezFlo dashboard: "CALLS ANSWERED" odometer, bars, a toggle, a cursor. Numbers must be real or flagged as placeholders.
- Applied so far in `precall-v2/` (Yousef's pre-call intro, first 16s).
