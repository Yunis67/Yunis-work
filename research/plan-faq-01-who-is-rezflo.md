# Plan · FAQ 01, "Who is RezFlo?" (43.8s)

The first of the FAQ videos that sit under the pre-call video on the page. Same room and same Yousef as the pre-call video, but this clip is real 1080p and much sharper, so we can push in on him without the picture going soft.

## The idea: a different look from the pre-call video

The pre-call video is a tech showreel: dark panels, timecode, REC dot, chapter counter. This video is about the people behind RezFlo, so it should feel more human. Think magazine profile or founder documentary instead of product demo.

- **Paper and glass.** Cream paper cards and frosted-glass cards (the wall shows through, blurred) instead of solid dark panels. Dark ink only for the dinner-rush cutaway.
- **No showreel HUD.** No timecode, REC or bar counter. Instead there's a small question tab top-left ("Q 01 · Who is RezFlo?"), which every FAQ video will share so they read as one series.
- **Same brand.** Brand purple for fills and accents, red only for missed calls, green only for answered calls and ticks. Same fonts as before: Archivo Black headlines, Instrument Serif italic accents, Inter UI, JetBrains Mono labels.
- **Things we haven't used yet:** freeze-frame photo print, map pin drop, camera punch-ins, frosted glass, an auto-reply email tearing in half, step-and-repeat echo stamps, a kitchen ticket rail, a "time stops" freeze, a text-message thread, and a split-flap board.

## Beats

**3.8–6.9 · "a small team based in Mississauga, Ontario" (scrapbook on the wall)**

## Transitions
- Camera punch-ins on emphasis (in on "not", "people who built it"; ease back out).
- A flash plus freeze-frame for the Polaroid.
- Hard cuts in and out of the two cutaways (kitchen, "not a pitch").
- Cards hand off to each other: echo stamps go into the pin, the kitchen phone card comes onto the wall, and the thread becomes the invite chip. Nothing pops out of nowhere.
- The corner tab grows into the end card.
