### 18.6–25.6 · split screen · counter, orders, kitchen, then the call
- **Camera push (C).** At 18.9 the footage slides left so Yousef sits left of centre (the plant crops out). The right 40% becomes a near-black panel with one soft purple glow behind the active tile.

## Production notes
- Built as one 46s composition (v2 0–16s plus this), so the hand-off at 16s is continuous. Rendered at 120fps and blended to 30fps for motion blur, same as v2.
- Sounds: kit sfx (tick, pop, snap, switch, chime, success, counter for the odometer). A soft phone ring generated in code (two-tone 440 + 480 Hz), kept low under his voice.
- The caller number is not shown. "FRI · 7:42 PM" and the ticket numbers are illustrative UI, not stats.
- Brand: Archivo Black / Instrument Serif italic / JetBrains Mono / Inter; ink, cream, brand purple; red only for the missed/lost call; green not used in this section.
